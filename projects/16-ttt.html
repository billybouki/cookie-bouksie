TTT

Augmented reality that enhances table tennis training.

Role TBD
Client TBD
Year TBD

Games, exercises, data collection, and fun routines are visualised directly on the table via a projector — an attempt at gamifying training and adding a new dimension to this beloved sport.

TTT started as Thomas Mayer's bachelor thesis and drew attention from media worldwide. It proceeded through the first rounds of funding from the EXIST program for innovative technologies, monitored by the TU. Many exciting things are on the way.
