Potential Drawer

An AI drawing tool, creating real-time interiors from visitors' drawings.

What

Potential Drawer is a multi-monitor and touchscreen-based installation, realised for the 2024 Milano Design Week, for Impress GmbH — another Intolight production. Visitors draw on the surface, and the engine transforms the drawings in near real time into full interior scenes, showcasing the palette of materials available from Impress surfaces.

How

A library of engineered and parametrised prompts was created, alongside colour palettes and inspiration images, to set a huge potential of outcomes from which a single drawing would be interpreted as a full scene.

Frame interpolation

Collection of liked visitor creations combined for the multi-monitor setup in the space.
