Potential Drawer

An AI-driven touchscreen brand experience generating imagery in real time.

What

A touchscreen table installation for a brand activation event, set beneath a wall sign reading "Imagine Your Potential." Visitors gather around the table as it generates imagery live, sourced from the brand's own visual world.

How

The table screen generates lifestyle scenes on the fly — interiors, objects, and moments styled in the brand's own palette — responding to visitors as they touch and browse. A companion phone experience lets visitors capture and take a version of what's generated with them.

On screen

Generated scenes lean into warm, everyday domestic moments — a cup of coffee on a couch, a made bed, a full shelf — rendered in the brand's colours and updated as visitors interact with the table.

Take it with you

A visitor photographs the table through the companion phone app, capturing their own generated result to keep.
