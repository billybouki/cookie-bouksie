A Surf game and a Forest exploration, played solely with movement. The user mounts a static surfboard or a treadmill, using it to navigate through levels, collecting featured products and points along the way — bringing a bit of physicality to gaming, and letting customers blow off some steam after disputing their phone charges.

Two multi-level 3D games occupying space in Germanos retail stores, played entirely through motion using tracking and sensor technologies.
